AI editing studio · transcript, captions, plan and Director Review

Speech edits keep the source audio and create separate projects. Transcription sends the first 120 seconds of one selected speech clip to OpenAI. Smart cuts and editing plans send text. Director Review sends up to 12 sampled rendered frames and measurements, not continuous audio/video. Each AI button makes a paid request; cached transcripts are reused.

Transcript editor

Checked words are kept. Reviewed selections are preserved during plan review. Uncheck text to remove it from the video. AI suggestions only change these checkboxes; review them before applying. Applying creates a separate cut from the original speech clip. Transcript timestamps are estimates.

Captions are burned into preview/export with a bottom safe margin. Edit caption text below and save. Changing trims or shot order clears captions on save; generate them again. Director Review checks sampled visuals, caption timing, pacing and measured audio levels; listen yourself to check dialogue and music balance.

Conversion sends caption text to AI in batches of up to 20 captions (one paid request per batch). Timing stays unchanged. Original restores the saved source captions without an AI request. Review translated text before export.
